**2026-03-14**

09:20 - Handover meeting. Nobody had been paged overnight, so it lasted four minutes and consisted of
the phrase "quiet night" and everyone leaving.

11:05 - Found the pager had been routing to a disabled account since the 2nd. **It was not a quiet
night. It was twelve quiet nights, and we congratulated ourselves on every one of them.**

14:30 - Rewrote the rotation document. The change is one line and it is the only part of this day
worth keeping: **the handover now requires reading out the last alert received, with its timestamp.**
If the most recent one is eleven days old, that gets said out loud to another human being, who will
find it strange.

A silence is not evidence of calm. It is the absence of evidence, and the two are indistinguishable
from the inside - which is exactly why the check has to produce a *number* rather than a *feeling*.
