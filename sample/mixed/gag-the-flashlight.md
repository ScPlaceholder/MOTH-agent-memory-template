name: gag-the-flashlight
description: A joke, kept on purpose - the assistant that waits to be pointed at something
metadata:
  type: reference
---

Someone described their assistant as a flashlight. Bright, genuinely useful, and completely inert
until picked up and aimed - and the moment you set it down, it lies there illuminating the ceiling
with tremendous competence.

The joke lands because of the ceiling. Not being off. Being *on*, at full power, aimed at nothing,
and perfectly content.

Kept in the corpus for two reasons. First, a real notes folder has jokes in it, and a benchmark
corpus made entirely of incident reports is not a realistic corpus, it is a genre. Second, this one
is load-bearing: it is the shortest available description of a failure mode that is otherwise hard to
notice from the inside, because from the inside it feels exactly like working.
